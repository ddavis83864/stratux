Weather Cache
Rolling FIS-B Weather Cache

Weather shown here is supplemental and may be incomplete or unavailable. Cached weather can be older than currently broadcast data - always check a product's own age before use. The absence of a cached product does not indicate the absence of a hazard. This is never a substitute for an official preflight briefing or current airborne weather sources.

This cache is disabled by default and only ever stores products genuinely received over 978 MHz FIS-B - never anything downloaded from the internet. It never deletes a recording, calibration profile, diagnostic bundle, or setting to make room.
