The Weather page shows FIS-B weather and aeronautical products this receiver has actually received over 978 MHz - a local, offline view, not a replacement for ForeFlight or an official weather briefing.

The tabs across the top separate products by type: METAR/SPECI, TAF, PIREP, Winds/Temps Aloft, NEXRAD, and Other (any decoded text report whose type isn't one of those). The NOTAM/SIGMET tab is a reception counter only - this receiver counts these frames but does not decode or retain their content.

Each product shows a freshness badge (fresh/aging/stale/expired) and its age. Age reflects how long ago the product was received or issued - it is not a statement that the weather itself is still valid for that long, and the absence of a product here never means the absence of a hazard.

Freshly-arrived reports appear immediately, live. Older cached products (received before this page was open) show their known metadata immediately; their raw text is recovered from disk automatically when the Weather Cache page's persistence setting is on, or can be requested with the "try loading from disk" link otherwise.

This page works with no Internet connection, over the Stratux Wi-Fi network. It relies on the Weather Cache page's own settings for how much history is kept - enable it there for the fullest picture; live reports appear here either way.

NOTE: reception depends on being within range of a 978 MHz ground station. On the ground or far from a tower, this page may show little or nothing - that reflects reception, not a receiver fault.
